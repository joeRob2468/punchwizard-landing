## Entries

**1. The Hat (_Pileus arcanus_).** Not clothing. A laminated horn of keratin grown from the crown, soft and felt-like at the surface. Cut through, it shows growth rings, one laid down each year of study, so a wizard's age can be read from a section of the hat. _Art:_ blue wash, drooping rippled brim, bent tip; rings in section.

**2. Conduit Lattice (_Rete pilei_).** Mana vessels that rise from the core through the Third Fontanelle and branch up through the hat to its tip, where surplus mana is discharged as the faint glow sometimes seen above a thinking wizard. _Art:_ cyan glow tubes, visible only in section.

**3. Third Fontanelle (_Fonticulus tertius_).** The opening at the top of the skull that closes in other species and never does in this one. The Conduit Lattice passes through it into the hat. _Art:_ a clean gap in the stippled vault on the midline.

**4. Aetheric Core (_Nucleus aethericus_).** A glowing nodule at the centre of the brain where mana is stored; every vessel leads to it. _Art:_ bright cyan sphere, pale at the heart.

**5. Ethereal Gland (_Glandula aetheris_).** A small double-lobed gland behind the core that meters out spell potency. When it is overworked the wizard suffers "burnout": dim eyes, a limp hat. _Art:_ ochre, faint glow.

**6. Focus Gems (_Gemmae cogitationis_).** Garnet-like crystals just under the cortex. One forms with each discipline mastered; old wizards carry many. _Art:_ garnet octahedra, glowing in section.

**7. Crystalline Eye (_Oculus crystallinus_).** Eyes fed directly by the vessels, able to see ley lines and the glow of enchanted things. _Art:_ softly glowing cyan.

**8. Incantatory Sinus (_Sinus incantatorius_).** A three-lobed resonance chamber behind the nose that tunes the voice for incantation; a blocked sinus spoils spells. _Art:_ verdigris, with resonance rings in section.

**9. Glossal Arcanum (_Lingua sigillata_).** A tongue with ridges like engraved sigils, built for the precise consonants of old spell-tongues. _Art:_ muscle red, ridged, hatched in section.

**10. Mnemonic Beard (_Barba mnemonica_).** Each hair holds one memory; trimming the beard is forgetting. Grows long, white and full. _Art:_ bone-white wash, flowing strands in section.

**11. Aetherial Vessels (_Vasa aetheria_).** Carry mana from the core down through the foramen and the neck to the shoulders and arms (for gesturing spells), and forward to the eyes. _Art:_ cyan glow tubes.

**12. Familiar's Perch (_Processus familiaris_).** A bony shelf grown out from the left clavicle, breaking through the robe, where a familiar (owl, toad, small cat) rides. _Art:_ bone, stippled in section; a small nub on the left shoulder from outside.

## Supporting structures

Unnumbered parts that frame the above: `head` (skin and flesh), `torso`, `skull`, `mandible`, `teeth`, `spine` (C1–T10), `discs`, `girdle` (clavicles), `sternum`, `scapulae`, `humeri`, `ribs`, `costal-cartilage`, `soft-palate`, `hyoid`, `larynx` (with the trachea), `oesophagus`, `meninges`, `brain`, `callosum`, `spinal-cord`, `hair`, `moustache`, `eyebrows`, `robe` and `mantle` with their fittings, and the hat's band, buckle and gem.
