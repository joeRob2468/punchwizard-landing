## Owner direction so far

- More realistic than cartoon: the face, eyes and proportions should follow the painted references (`images (4).jpg`) rather than strict anatomy; eyes large and readable, not hooded shut.

- A possible later step: dark background, more monotone, internals like an MRI scan (not decided); `images (5).jpg` and `gettyimages-2230129228-612x612.jpg` show that look.
